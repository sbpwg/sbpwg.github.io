# SBPW2 Propagation Test Cases

**Pressure should be interpolated hydro-statically. An email was sent to participants to clarify the interpretation of the provided pressure lookup tables. Contact aiaa-boompw-committee@lists.nasa.gov for clarification.**

The following test cases and data are being requested from participants in the atmospheric propagation part of the Sonic Boom Prediction Workshop. Participants are encouraged to apply their best practices for computing the ground signatures and other requested data for the provided cases.

There are two required cases with optional opportunities for additional analysis

- [Axi-symmetric Body of Revolution Propagation](axibody/), required
- [Lockheed Martin 1021 Propagation](lm1021/), required
