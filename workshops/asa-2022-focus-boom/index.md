# ASA Focus Boom (2022)

ASA 2022 Special Focus Boom Session

version 2, 08-MAR-2022

A special sonic boom focus prediction session is planned as part of the 182nd Acoustical Society of America (ASA) meeting to be held from 23-27 May 2022 at the Sheraton Denver Downtown Hotel in Denver, Colorado. The session is intended to cover the state-of-the-art for predicting focused booms on the ground under aircraft accelerations. Near-field waveforms would be provided and participants are encouraged to apply their best practices for predicting sonic boom ground signatures and thier associated metrics for the cases provided.

Important Dates:

- 24 January 2022: Due date for ASA abstracts
- 23 May 2022: Submission of results to the NASA Box file share location.
- 23-27 May 2022: 182nd ASA Meeting

The Third Sonic Boom Prediction Workshop Propagation Presentation Guidelines (Version 2) will be reused for ASA 2022: [SBPW3_Propagation_Presentation_Guidelines_v2.pdf](/references/sbpw3/SBPW3_Propagation_Presentation_Guidelines_v2.pdf)

The test cases are in the case1 and case2 directories.

- <https://lbpw-ftp.larc.nasa.gov/asa-2022-focus-boom/case1/>
- <https://lbpw-ftp.larc.nasa.gov/asa-2022-focus-boom/case2/>

See the files Focusing_Case1_Spec_v0.txt and Focusing_Case2_Spec_v0.txt in these directories for details.
