# 2016 AIAA SciTech Special Sessions

These are the papers for the sonic boom activities special session at AIAA SciTech 2016.

## Special Session: Low Boom Activities

[A New F-Function for the Low-Boom Aircraft Design with Trim Requirement (AIAA 2016-2032)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2032)\
Yohei Kasuga, Kenji Yoshida, Hiroaki Ishikawa

[Multi-fidelity Low-boom Design Based on Near-field Pressure Signature (AIAA 2016-2033)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2033)\

[Computational and Experimental Study of Supersonic Nozzle Flow and Aft-Deck Interaction (AIAA 2016-2034)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2034)\

[Wind Tunnel Model Design for Sonic Boom Studies of Nozzle Jet Flow with Shock Interactions (AIAA 2016-2035)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2035)\
Susan E. Cliff, Marie F. Denison, Shayan Moini-Yekta, Donald E. Morr, Donald A. Durston

[Numerical Examination of Shock Generator Geometries and Nozzle Plume Effects on Pressure Signature (AIAA 2016-2036)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2036)\

[A Space Marching Method for Sonic Boom Near Field Predictions (AIAA 2016-2037)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2016-2037)\
