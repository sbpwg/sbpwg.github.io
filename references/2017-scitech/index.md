# 2017 AIAA SciTech Special Sessions

These are the papers for the sonic boom activities special session at AIAA SciTech 2017.

## Special Session: Low Boom Activities I

[Near Field Sonic Boom Analysis with HUNS3D Solver (AIAA 2017-0038)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0038)\
Boping Ma, Gang Wang, Jiong Ren, Zhengyin Ye, and Gecheng Zha

[Wing Planform Optimization Method for Low-Boom and Low-Drag Aircraft (AIAA 2017-0039)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0039)\
Yohei Kasuga, Kenji Yoshida, and Hiroaki Ishikawa

[Impact of Aeroelastic Uncertainties on Sonic Boom Signature of a Commercial Supersonic Transport Configuration (AIAA 2017-0040)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0040)\
Melike Nikbay, Bret Stanford, Thomas K. West, and Sriram K. Rallabhandi

[Nozzle Plume/Shock Interaction Sonic Boom Test Results from the NASA Ames 9- by 7-Foot Supersonic Wind Tunnel (AIAA 2017-0041)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0041)\
Donald A. Durston, Susan E. Cliff, Marie Denison, Derek Dalle, Raymond S. Castner, Alaa A. Elmiligui, Melissa B. Carter, Courtney Winski, Patrick R. Shea, Brennan Blumenthal, and Nathanial T. Smith

[Computational Evaluations of Experimental Data for Sonic Boom Models with Nozzle Jet Flow Interactions (AIAA 2017-0042)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0042)\
James C. Jensen, Marie Denison, Don Durston, and Susan E. Cliff

[Retroreflective Background-Oriented Schlieren Imaging Results from the NASA Ames Plume-Shock Interaction Test (AIAA 2017-0043)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0043)\
Nathanial T. Smith, Donald Durston, and James T. Heineck

[Numerical Evauation of Effect of Atmospheric Turbulence on Sonic Boom Observed in D-SEND#2 Flight Test (AIAA 2017-0278)](http://arc.aiaa.org/doi/abs/10.2514/6.2017-0278)\
