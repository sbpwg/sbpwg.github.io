# 2015 AIAA SciTech Special Sessions

These are the papers for the two sonic boom activities special sessions at AIAA SciTech 2015. Some of the [Oral Presentations](https://lbpw-ftp.larc.nasa.gov/scitech-2015/ "Oral Presentations") from this special session are available on the FTP server.
